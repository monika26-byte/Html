🍕 Homemade Pizza Recipe

Pizza is a delicious Italian dish made with dough, pizza sauce, cheese, and different toppings. You can make it at home easily.

Ingredients

For Pizza Dough

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1 teaspoon yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 1 tablespoon oil

For Pizza Topping

  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 capsicum, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1/2 cup sweet corn
  • Olives (optional)
  • Chilli flakes
  • Oregano seasoning
  • Black pepper (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add warm water and oil, then knead into a dough.
  3. Cover the dough and let it rise for about 1 hour.
  4. Roll the dough into a round pizza base.
  5. Spread pizza sauce over the base.
  6. Add mozzarella cheese and your favorite vegetables.
  7. Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes.
  8. Bake at 220°C for about 15 minutes, until cooked.
  9. Let it cool slightly, slice, and serve!

Tip: Add extra cheese for a cheesy pizza! Enjoy your homemade pizza!
